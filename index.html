Safari Media Grabber v3.0

Find publicly accessible media on webpages, inspect HLS playlists, select quality and audio, download in chunks, then merge locally in Safari.

Limitations: No DRM, access-control, CORS, or iframe security bypass. Bookmarklet detection is best-effort. Safari Web Inspector can expose requests a bookmarklet cannot. Only save content you are allowed to keep. Files remain in your browser, not uploaded to this site.

1 · Discover media

Use the included Safari bookmarklet on the original page, then paste its results here. Alternatively paste a copied .m3u8 or .mp4 URL from Safari Network, or upload downloaded .m3u8 files.

2 · Choose tracks

Inspect playlists to discover matching tracks.

Ready.

3 · Combine video + audio

If you already have MP4 tracks, choose them here. Otherwise downloaded tracks are used automatically. Large files can exhaust Safari memory.
